### New

- Encore's first release. It grew out of Klangbild and keeps its calm look, now laid out as a player.
- The lyrics stand large and bold beside the cover, line by line as they're sung, and the lines further away go softly out of focus.
- The cover's own colors fill the background from the start.
- Two buttons under the progress bar turn the lyrics on and off and open the settings.
- A new logo and app icon.
